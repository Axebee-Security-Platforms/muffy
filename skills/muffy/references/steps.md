(2) Copy each `drawn.graph.json` to `.team/<task>/diagrams/fe.graph.json` / `be.graph.json` (the reviewer is read-only and cannot open a URL; step 6 reads these files). Missing diagram → skip, never block. Details: [pr-diagram.md](../../../agents/references/frontend-dev/pr-diagram.md) |
